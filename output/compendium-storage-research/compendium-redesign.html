Recommended order

First remove rebuilds from the normal browsing path. Then compare small server pages with a cached browser catalogue. A whole-category download uses more data on a first visit; it must earn its place through measured improvements.

Design exploration only. No app changes or release. Local CPU measurements do not establish an end-to-end website loading time.
